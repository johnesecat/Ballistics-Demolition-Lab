# Visual asset inventory

`public/assets/ammo/icons/` contains 59 original transparent illustrations, and `public/assets/ammo/rounds/` contains the corresponding low-resolution pixel sprites. Both are drawn independently by `scripts/extract_icons.py` from the same editable geometry recipes: flight art is not sampled from the icon or from reference images. `docs/ammo-contact-sheet.png` shows the card art at 96 × 40 CSS pixels per illustration. No shell lettering or source-image pixels are copied.

The screenshots in `example images/` are reference material and are not loaded by the game. Their visible silhouettes, colors and components influenced the drawings. The following requested designs had a described visible appearance: AP Solid Shot, APC Capped, APBC Ballistic Cap, APCBC Combined Caps, APCR Round, Petal, Bonded Mushroom, Crusher, Heavy Soft Shot, HEAT Round, HESH Round, Broadhead, Scab Lifter, Shock, HESH Demolition, Capped APHE, Bunkerbreaker, PELE Sidewinder, Wedge, Bankshot, Ribbon HESH, Echo HESH, Rainmaker, Crownburst, Obstacle Reducer, Latchburst, Adaptive Shell, Drillhead, Splitter and Needle Jet. All details in these new drawings are interpretations, not extracted originals. Remaining icon designs are inferred from the supplied descriptions. The extra Strong Jet card is not included in the requested 59-round catalogue.

The cannon, backgrounds, core, material surfaces and debris textures are original code-generated pixel art. Music and effects are original synthesized Web Audio; no sound is claimed to match the source. Fonts are locally bundled from Fontsource.
